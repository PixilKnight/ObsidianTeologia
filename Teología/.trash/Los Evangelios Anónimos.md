Papyrus 4 (BnF Suppl. gr. 1120 ii 3) "El Evangelio Según Mateo"
​	Fragmentary flyleaf for a manuscript of the Gospel of Matthew, containing the title ευαγγελιον κ̣ατ̣α μαθ᾽θαιον (euangelion kata Maththaion). Part of BnF Suppl. gr. 1120 ii 3 (= Gregory-Aland P4, Papyrus 4). This is the earliest known manuscript title for Matthew's gospel and one of the earliest manuscript titles for any gospel (alongside with John's P66 and P75).
From the late 2nd century or early 3rd century (or 150–175 C.E) S. Gathercole (2012). _The Earliest Manuscript Title of Matthew’s Gospel (BnF Suppl. gr. 1120 ii 3 / P4)_, Novum Testamentum, 54, 209-235. There is no counterevidence from any manuscript tradition suggesting an anonymous Matthew or an alternative attribution.

Papyrus 66 (Gregory-Aland) P. Bodmer II "El Evangelio Según Juan"
​	Greek manuscript of the New Testament. Circa 200 C.E or 125-150 C.E. "Εὐαγγέλιον  
κατὰ Ἰωάννην" -> "Gospel according to John". This title is written in the same hand as the main text, using the same ink and letter forms. There is no evidence of later correction, supplementation, or editorial interference.

Papyrus 75 (formerly Papyrus Bodmer XIV–XV, now Hanna Papyrus 1) "El Evangelio Según Lucas"
​	An early Greek New Testament manuscript written on papyrus containing text from the Gospel of Luke 3:18–24:53, and John 1:1–15:8. Circa 175–225 C.E. "Εὐαγγέλιον κατὰ Λουκᾶν" -> "Gospel according to Luke". This subscription appears after the narrative of Luke concludes.
